# PauseGuard V5.0 — Future Product & Engineering Backlog

> **Scope Notice:** This backlog documents proposed **V5.0** enhancements only. None of these features are implemented in PauseGuard V4.1, which remains a synthetic, mock-rail UPI APP-fraud intervention prototype. No real-money execution, live KYC collection, or production banking integration is active in V4.1.

| # | Feature | User Problem | Proposed Architecture | Dependencies | Owner | Priority | Security Considerations | Acceptance Criteria | External Auth / Provider Required |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **OTP Verification Provider Integration** | V4.1 uses synthetic demo sessions without real SMS/email challenges. | Server-side OTP challenge service with rate-limited verification codes and short TTLs. | Auth router, SMS/Email gateway | Member 1 (Komal) | P0 | Hash OTPs at rest; enforce strict per-actor and per-IP rate limits; never log codes. | Expired/wrong OTPs fail closed; replay rejected. | **Yes** (SMS/Email provider credentials) |
| 2 | **Trusted Bank Identity / KYC Assurance Adapter** | Prototype identities are synthetic and unverified against bank KYC. | Read-only bank/PSP KYC assurance token verifier issuing signed `TrustedActorContext` claims. | Auth service, PSP OAuth/OIDC | Member 1 / Member 4 | P0 | Store zero raw Aadhaar/PAN data; verify cryptographic issuer signatures only. | Only verified KYC assertions map to `PAYER`/`GUARDIAN` roles. | **Yes** (Regulated bank/PSP partner) |
| 3 | **Duplicate-Identity & Self-Guardianship Prevention** | A coerced payer could register a second account or device as their own guardian. | Cross-account identity graph & device-fingerprint deduplication check at enrollment. | Enrollment service, KYC assurance | Member 4 (Jeevan) | P0 | Compare salted cryptographic identity hashes; reject shared KYC/device bindings. | Self-enrollment and shared-KYC guardian pairs rejected with 403/409. | **Yes** (KYC assurance attributes) |

| 7 | **Guardian Compromise & Collusion Handling** | A compromised or colluding guardian account could approve coerced transfers. | Anomaly scoring on guardian approval latency, device changes, and shared IP/network signals. | Intelligence orchestrator, guardian service | Member 3 / Member 4 | P1 | Suspicious guardian approvals trigger step-up hold rather than `READY_TO_EXECUTE`. | Collusion indicators block transition to `READY_TO_EXECUTE` and log audit alert. | **No** |

| 9 | **Durable Notification Outbox** | In-memory WebSocket broadcasts can be lost if the process restarts post-commit. | Transactional outbox table (`notification_outbox`) written in the same DB transaction and drained by a worker. | PostgreSQL, Alembic, event dispatcher | Member 1 (Komal) | P0 | Outbox worker must only dispatch committed rows and guarantee at-least-once idempotent delivery. | Process crash after commit still delivers pending WS/push events on recovery. | **No** |

| 11 | **Legitimate-Payment Retry & Emergency Escalation** | Users need a safe way to retry after a false alarm without weakening anti-smurfing. | Explicit cooldown-bound retry token tied to verified step-up challenge. | Durable payment engine, anti-smurfing | Member 1 / Member 4 | P2 | Retries must still count toward rolling velocity unless cleared by verified step-up. | Retried payment preserves full audit lineage to original paused payment. | **No** |
